Memo Calculator: Keep Results

A calculator that lets you name your results, keep them as memos, and bring them back into the calculator later. On your Home Screen it appears as "Memo Calc".

FAQ

How do I save a result as a memo?

Type a name in the name field ("What's this?"), then drag the big number on the display up to the memo pad and let go. You can drop it anywhere in the list. Tapping the ↑ button at the left of the display adds it to the end of the memos. You can also save without a name.

When you turn your phone sideways, or on iPad, the memo pad and the calculator sit side by side. Drag the display toward the memo pad.

How do I use a memo's number in a calculation?

Tap the ↓ button on the right of the memo, or press and hold the memo, carry it over the calculator and let go. After an operator such as "+", it is added to the calculation. The memo's name appears above its number in the calculation.

How do I edit, reorder or delete memos?

Tap a memo to change its name and number, or to delete it. Right after deleting, you can bring it back with "Undo". Press and hold a memo to pick it up and move it.

How do I add or remove tax?

In Settings (the gear at the top right of the memo pad), under "Tax keys (TAX+ / TAX−)", choose "Show the keys". The keys appear to the right of the name field. You can also change the "Tax rate".

How do I round decimals?

Under "Decimal rounding" in Settings, choose how many digits to keep and how to round (round half up, round down or round up).

How do I remove ads?

Buy "Remove ads" under "Ads" in Settings. It is a one-time purchase, so ads never come back. After changing devices or reinstalling the App, tap "Restore purchase" with the same Apple Account.

Where are my memos stored?

Memos are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere. Deleting the App also deletes your memos.

How do I change the key sound or vibration?

Under "When you press a key" in Settings, turn "Sound" and "Vibration" on or off. No sound plays while your iPhone or iPad is in silent mode.

How do I change the look or the language?

Choose a skin under "Theme" in Settings (all skins are free). Under "Language", choose 日本語, English or 繁體中文. "Auto" follows your device's language.

How do I see the guide again?

In Settings, under "How to use", tap "Open the guide".
