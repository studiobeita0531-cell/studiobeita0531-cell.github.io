FAQ

I'm stuck

Tap the light bulb at the top right to see hints for where you are, one at a time. The last hint shows the answer.

How do I use items?

Tap an item at the bottom of the screen to select it, then tap the spot where you want to use it. Tap a selected item again to examine it closely.

Can I stop in the middle?

Your progress is saved automatically. Choose "Continue" on the title screen to pick up where you left off.

There is no sound

Check the volume settings in the menu (top left). On iPhone, no sound plays while the silent switch is on. On Android, check your device's media volume.

How do I change the language?

Open the menu (or Settings on the title screen) and choose a language under "Language". "Automatic" follows your device's language.

About ads

Ads are shown at the bottom of the screen. Watching a video ad unlocks each new hint. For details, see the Privacy Policy.
